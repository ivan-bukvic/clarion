Ridgeline Renovations — Scope of Work
Project: Miller Residence Kitchen Remodel
Address: 214 Maple Street, Unit B
Client: Robert Miller
Prepared by: Sarah Whitfield, Project Lead

Scope of Work:
- Demolition of existing cabinetry and countertops
- Installation of new custom cabinets (shaker style, white oak finish)
- Installation of quartz countertops
- New tile backsplash
- Electrical rough-in for under-cabinet lighting
- Plumbing reconnection for sink and dishwasher

Budget and Timeline:
The total estimated cost for this project is $18,750, including materials and labor.
The estimated completion timeline is 6 weeks from the start date, weather and permit approval permitting.
Estimated start date: April 6th.

Exclusions:
- Appliance purchase and delivery (owner-furnished)
- Flooring replacement outside the kitchen footprint
- Structural modifications beyond cabinet wall blocking
